## Most consequential events (seed 1)

Score = men lost in the event (killed and wounded by its fire or mine, on both sides; men captured by its surrender, trapping or clearing; the garrison lost to a bombardment result) + 100 × the trench segments and objective lines changing hands (segment-lost, segment-retaken, line-taken, line-lost) whose refs lead back to it within 6 levels, the change itself included. Batch extracts keep no events, so this reads the explained run only.

**Caveat.** The ranking is lopsided. A ground change is credited to every event its refs lead back to, but men lost count only on the event itself. Bookkeeping events on the way to a taking (barrage lifts, companies reaching a line, orders) therefore rank high while losing no men, and the causes that sit on nearly every attacking chain (`timetable`, `followed-barrage`) top the cause counts without telling one event from another. Fire is logged per target company and per step, and direct-fire events carry no refs to their firers, so a single fire event seldom reaches 100 men and no chain gathers a firer's losses together: fire losses are under-ranked here, however many men they cost in all. Grouping losses by firer needs firer ids on fire events (a Plan 4 item). Read this section as "what moved the ground", not "what cost the men".
